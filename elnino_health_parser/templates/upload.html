How extraction works

  1. Digital PDFs: text + tables via PyMuPDF and pdfplumber.
  2. Scanned PDFs / photos: pages are rendered and read with RapidOCR.
  3. Disease names, Maharashtra districts, case and death counts are matched with regex + spaCy.
  4. Records are stored in SQLite and shown on the dashboard.

Scanned files can take 20–60 seconds. Keep this tab open.
