Maharashtra Public Health Department
Disease Surveillance Bulletin – July 2025
Aarogya Patrika Special Issue: Climate-Sensitive Diseases

Report Date: 15/07/2025

Summary of El Niño-related disease activity across districts:

Dengue
Pune district reported 156 positive cases and 3 deaths during the reporting period.
Mumbai recorded 210 Dengue cases with 5 fatalities.
Nagpur: 89 cases, 1 death.
Thane reported 134 Dengue cases and 2 deaths.

Malaria
Gadchiroli continues to report elevated malaria incidence with 67 cases.
Nashik: 45 malaria cases, 1 death.
Amravati reported 38 cases of malaria with no deaths.

Chikungunya
Pune: 72 cases of Chikungunya.
Kolhapur district recorded 41 Chikungunya cases.

Cholera
Solapur reported 12 cholera cases. No deaths.

Leptospirosis
Mumbai: 28 Leptospirosis cases and 1 death linked to heavy rainfall.
Raigad: 19 cases of Leptospirosis.

Heat Stroke
Ahmednagar: 34 heat stroke cases, 4 deaths during prolonged heatwave.
Jalna: 21 cases, 2 deaths.
Aurangabad recorded 47 Heat Stroke cases and 6 fatalities in May-June period.

This bulletin is issued for public health preparedness under the Integrated Disease Surveillance Programme (IDSP).
Contact: State Surveillance Unit, Mumbai.
